🇬🇧 RuSwitcher — a free keyboard-layout switcher for macOS (an open-source PuntoSwitcher alternative). Open source, no telemetry, 16 UI languages.

⬇️ Install:
• Homebrew: brew tap rashn/ruswitcher && brew install --cask ruswitcher
• or DMG: https://github.com/rashn/RuSwitcher/releases

🐛 Bugs & ideas: https://github.com/rashn/RuSwitcher/issues — or in the discussion chat.
🧪 Beta versions: Settings → Advanced → “Get beta versions”.

This channel is for news & releases. The discussion chat is for questions & support — write in any language.
